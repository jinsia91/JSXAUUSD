How the signal is worked out

The first 1–5 minute spike often reverses. Spreads on CPT Markets widen around NFP, CPI and FOMC, so stops placed tight before the release can be hit by spread alone. Revisions to the previous figure and the details (wages for NFP, core for CPI) can flip the headline reaction.

XAUUSD fundamentals · live drivers

  • Yields ▲Higher US real yields raise the cost of holding gold. Rising 10Y with a rising dollar is the most bearish mix.
  • Fed cutsRate-cut expectations weaken the dollar and lift gold. Hawkish surprises do the opposite.
  • Risk-offSharp stock selloffs, wars and tariff shocks bring safe-haven buying into gold.
  • Central banksSteady central-bank and ETF buying supports dips over weeks, not minutes.

DXY · US Dollar Index

Inverse to gold
  • DXY ▲Stronger dollar makes gold more expensive abroad. Gold usually falls.
  • DXY ▼Weaker dollar usually lifts gold. Watch DXY breaking its prior day high or low as confirmation.
  • Both ▲Gold and DXY rising together signals fear-driven safe-haven buying. Treat gold dips as buyable.

USOIL · WTI crude

Inflation & risk gauge
  • Oil spikeSupply shock or Middle East / Russia tension. Raises inflation fears and safe-haven demand. Supportive for gold.
  • Oil slumpLower inflation expectations. Mildly bearish gold, unless the drop comes from recession fear.
  • WatchEIA inventories (Wednesday, US time), OPEC+ meetings, sanctions headlines and Trump posts about oil prices or OPEC.
